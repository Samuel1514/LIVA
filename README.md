# LIVA
LIVA - A Virtual Assistant for Older Adults  LIVA is an interactive platform designed to alleviate loneliness among older people by providing a virtual avatar that engages in meaningful interactions.The platform offers a range of essential services, including:

	•	Medication Notifications: Timely reminders for taking prescribed medications.
	•	Medical Assistance: Alerts and notifications for health checkups or emergencies.
	•	Meal Reminders: Scheduled reminders for meal times.
	•	Health Monitoring: Continuous monitoring of health parameters with actionable insights.

LIVA aims to support older adults, especially those whose children or family members live far away, ensuring their well-being and fostering a sense of connection.
